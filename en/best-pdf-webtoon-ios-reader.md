A PDF webtoon can be difficult to read when its pages contain white borders, paired scans or mismatched image widths. In Manga Capsule, start with vertical scrolling, then inspect the page layout before changing cropping or spread settings.

**Published by the Manga Capsule team, updated October 13, 2026.** This is a guide to our reader's controls. We have not conducted a controlled multi-app webtoon test and do not assign competitor scores or fixed opening times. For documented reader alternatives, see the [five-reader comparison](/en/blog/ios-manga-reader-recommendations).

## Start with the actual PDF layout

A comic originally drawn as a continuous vertical strip differs from a scanned print comic with two pages per PDF sheet. Check a few representative pages first. A portrait page, a wide spread and a very tall image can need different display choices.

| What you see | Setting to try | Check the result |
|---|---|---|
| Long vertical images | Vertical continuous scroll and width fit | Text remains readable without repeated zooming |
| Large white borders | Margin cropping | Edge captions and speech bubbles remain visible |
| Two scanned pages inside one PDF sheet | Split-page controls | Left/right order matches the book |
| Two separate pages forming a spread | Two-page layout in landscape | The center of the spread aligns correctly |
| Inconsistent widths between pages | Adjust fitting and cropping | A narrow page is not stretched beyond a useful size |

## Use vertical scrolling for a vertical comic

Open a book and use the reader's layout controls to choose a vertical reading mode. If automatic recognition produces a layout you do not want, adjust the mode manually. Scroll across several page boundaries and check whether blank space comes from the reader or is part of the source image.

Cropping can reduce unwanted borders, but it cannot reliably distinguish every intentional pause or gutter in the artwork. Avoid assuming every white area should disappear. For more context, see the [webtoon seam guide](/en/blog/webtoon-seam-problem).

## Split a scanned spread or show a real spread

Splitting an image containing two pages is different from displaying two single pages next to one another. On a phone, split display can make each half readable. On an iPad in landscape, a two-page layout can preserve the composition of paired pages.

Inspect the cover and an interior spread after changing the setting. The best left/right order depends on the book's reading direction. See [split and merge controls](/en/blog/manga-double-page-split-merge).

## Crop conservatively

Try automatic margin cropping on a bordered scan, then check pages with text near the edge. Switch it off or adjust it if content disappears. The amount of screen area saved depends on the PDF; there is no universal percentage improvement. See [PDF white-margin settings](/en/blog/pdf-manga-white-margin).

## Understand loading and storage

Manga Capsule supports PDF reading, but a remote PDF does not always stream. Mounted iCloud files, PDFs from some cloud services, encrypted PDFs and PDFs that cannot be parsed remotely follow a local-download path. Downloaded books and reading caches use device storage. Opening time varies with the file, connection, source and device.

For offline reading, download the volume fully and test with networking off. A previously opened remote book does not guarantee every page is cached. Free local reading allows five books per week; network shelves and streaming require Pro. Check the app for monthly, yearly and lifetime options at your regional price.

## Choose by a small real-file check

Try a long-image PDF, a bordered scan and a spread on your own device. Check legibility, page order, scroll behavior and offline reopening. If comparing apps, record their versions, your device, file and cache state rather than treating a marketing table as a benchmark.
