# Best iOS Manga Reader for PDF Webtoons: Vertical Manga Experience Review (2026)

Webtoons and Chinese vertical manga have been exploding in popularity. *The Beginning After the End*, *Solo Leveling*, *Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint*, *The Hidden Dungeon* — these works share one thing in common: **they're drawn vertically**.

What does drawing vertically mean? It means traditional manga readers (horizontal page turns, left→right or right→left) are an absolute disaster for webtoons — images get squashed, huge black bars appear at top and bottom, page-turn logic breaks down, and scrolling stutters.

Even more frustrating, many webtoon resources come in **PDF format** — scanned double-page PDFs, entire volume exports, or long-image PDFs scraped from online reading platforms. iOS's native Books and Files apps either display these PDFs at completely wrong ratios or lag for seconds every time you turn a page.

**If you mainly read PDF webtoons, this article is for you.**

---

## Three Pain Points of Reading Webtoons in PDF

Before diving into the comparison, let's clearly define the problem. PDF webtoons have three issues that almost no general-purpose reader handles well:

### Pain Point 1: Aspect Ratio Mismatch

PDF pages are typically A4 or Letter ratio (wider than tall), but webtoons have extreme vertical ratios. Scaling to fit width leaves huge blank spaces at top and bottom; fitting to height makes the image blurry.

**Required feature**: Automatic white margin cropping, intelligent width fitting.

### Pain Point 2: Scanned Double-Page Splitting

Many PDF webtoons are scanned — one PDF page contains two manga pages (dual-page scan). This is barely tolerable on iPad in landscape, but completely unreadable on iPhone.

**Required feature**: Intelligent page splitting, automatically separating double-page scans into individual pages.

### Pain Point 3: Discontinuous Scrolling

The correct way to read webtoons is **continuous vertical scrolling**, not horizontal page turns. PDF files are paginated, and if the reader turns pages one by one, it pauses at the bottom of each page, completely breaking the reading rhythm.

**Required feature**: Continuous scroll mode (scroll mode), seamless page transitions.
